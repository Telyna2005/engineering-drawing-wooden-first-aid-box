# DESIGN AND FABRICATION OF A WOODEN FIRST AID BOX


## 1. INTRODUCTION

A first aid box is a useful storage container for medicines, bandages, cotton, antiseptic materials and other basic medical supplies. In this project, a compact **wooden first aid box** was designed and fabricated using engineering drawing principles and basic workshop operations.

The project involved several manufacturing processes, including **measurement, marking, cutting, drilling, sewing, joining, sanding and finishing**. The engineering drawing was used as the basic reference for determining the dimensions and arrangement of the different components.

The main purpose of this project was to understand how a two-dimensional engineering drawing can be converted into a practical three-dimensional product using suitable materials and manufacturing techniques.

---

## 2. OBJECTIVES

The objectives of the project are:

* To design a simple and functional wooden first aid box.
* To apply engineering drawing and dimensional measurement principles.
* To gain practical experience in wood cutting and drilling.
* To learn basic sewing for making a handle or internal storage pouch.
* To understand joining and assembly techniques.
* To develop skills in sanding and surface finishing.
* To manufacture a safe, portable and useful first aid box.

## 4. DESIGN AND ENGINEERING DRAWING

Before fabrication, the first aid box was designed using engineering drawing principles. The basic design consisted of a rectangular wooden box with a hinged top cover and an internal partition for organizing first aid materials.

The drawing was prepared using **front, top and side views**, with dimensions marked clearly. The positions of the hinges, handle supports, screws and internal partition were also considered during the design stage.

## 5. FABRICATION PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Measurement and Marking

The required dimensions were transferred from the engineering drawing onto the wooden sheet using a steel rule, pencil and try square. The try square was used to maintain accurate 90° corners.

All cutting lines and drilling points were marked carefully to reduce errors and material wastage.

### 5.2 Cutting

The marked wooden sheet was cut into individual components using a hand saw or jigsaw.

The following components were prepared:

* Bottom panel
* Front panel
* Rear panel
* Two side panels
* Top cover
* Internal partition
* Wooden supports

After cutting, each component was checked against the required dimensions.

### 5.3 Drilling

Drilling was carried out to provide holes for screws, hinges and the handle arrangement.

The drilling points were marked before using the drill machine. A suitable drill bit was selected according to the screw size. The wooden pieces were held firmly while drilling to prevent movement.

After drilling, wood chips and rough edges around the holes were removed. Countersinking was carried out where required so that screw heads would not protrude from the surface.

### 5.4 Assembly and Joining

The wooden panels were assembled to form the main box. Wood adhesive was applied to the joining surfaces and the panels were aligned properly.

Screws were then used to strengthen the joints. The internal partition was fixed inside the box after checking that it did not interfere with the movement of the lid.

Two hinges were attached between the box and the top cover. Pilot holes were drilled before fixing the hinges with screws. The lid was then tested several times to ensure smooth opening and closing.

### 5.5 Sewing and Handle Preparation

A fabric or nylon-webbing handle was prepared to make the box portable. The required length of material was measured and cut.

The ends were folded to prevent fraying and were stitched securely using strong thread. The stitching was checked for strength before attaching the handle to the wooden box.

The handle was fixed to the box using suitable screws and supports. Alternatively, the fabric could be used to create a small internal pouch for storing smaller first aid items.

### 5.6 Sanding

After assembly, all wooden surfaces and edges were sanded to remove splinters, rough edges and surface irregularities.

Coarse sandpaper was used initially, followed by finer sandpaper to obtain a smoother surface. Particular attention was given to the corners and edges because the box would be handled frequently.

The box was cleaned thoroughly after sanding to remove wood dust.

### 5.7 Finishing

The final finishing process was carried out to improve the appearance and protect the wood.

The surface was cleaned and a suitable primer or wood sealer was applied if required. After drying, paint, wood stain or varnish was applied uniformly.

Additional coats were applied where necessary to obtain a smooth and durable finish.

---

## 6. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

The following safety precautions were followed during fabrication:

* Safety goggles were used during cutting and drilling.
* The wooden workpiece was held firmly while operating tools.
* Hands were kept away from saw blades and drill bits.
* Tools were used only for their intended purpose.
* The working area was kept clean and free from wood chips.
* Care was taken while handling screws and sharp tools.
* Paint, varnish and adhesives were used in a well-ventilated area.
* All sharp wooden edges and exposed screw tips were removed or covered.

---

## 7. FINAL INSPECTION

After completing the fabrication, the first aid box was inspected for:

* Dimensional accuracy
* Strength of wooden joints
* Proper alignment of the lid
* Smooth operation of hinges
* Strength of the sewn handle
* Properly drilled and tightened screws
* Smooth and safe wooden surfaces
* Uniform finishing

The final product was found to be functional, portable and suitable for storing basic first aid materials.

A functional **wooden first aid box** was successfully designed and fabricated using engineering drawing and basic workshop techniques.

The project included the complete manufacturing process of **measurement, marking, cutting, drilling, sewing, joining, sanding and finishing**. The final box was provided with a hinged lid, handle and internal storage arrangement.
